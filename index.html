Permission Level 0–3

0 · Read only: stok, order, histori. 1 · Safe action dengan log: catat lead, draf invoice, rangkum chat. 2 · Approval: invoice resmi, stok manual, driver, publikasi. 3 · Owner only: refund, harga, hapus pelanggan, transfer, dan budget besar.
